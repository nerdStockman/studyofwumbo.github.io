Calculation details

Model assumptions: uniform solid lead; a square-based pyramid, apex upward, with a flat horizontal base; an aligned solid sphere; Newtonian gravity; no electrical, magnetic, aerodynamic, or balance effects. The side view shows a triangle, but the mass calculation uses the full 3D square pyramid. The pyramid shape ratio is an estimate, not a measurement. Numerical integration uses midpoint sampling.
